# AI Claim Tracker

Viral AI discoveries and creations, linked to their sources.

Sister site to [Fast, With AI](https://woletee.github.io/fast-with-ai/).

## Submitting an entry

1. Anyone opens **[Submit an entry](https://github.com/woletee/ai-claim-tracker/issues/new?template=new-claim.yml)** and fills in the short form (about 3 minutes).
2. A bot checks the form and the links, then comments on the issue.
3. A maintainer reviews the sources and adds the `approved` label.
4. The bot adds the entry to `data/claims.json`, saves the source's preview image as the card image, credits it, and closes the issue. The site updates in a minute or two.

Card images are each source's own preview image and belong to their owners.
